About This Service

Safe, Secure Storage

When you are moving houses or between homes and need a place to store your things, our storage facilities are here for you. All our units are clean and dry, and we control the temperature so your belongings are not damaged by extreme heat or cold.

We have storage solutions to suit every need. You can rent a small locker if you only have a few boxes, or a large unit if you have a lot of things, such as the full contents of a 4-bedroom house. You can access your unit any time you want, and if you need us to, we can collect your things and bring them to the unit for you.

All our storage facilities are well protected. We have cameras watching at all times, alarm systems, and secure locks on every door. Your belongings are fully insured while in our care, and our team is always available to help if you have any questions or need access to your unit.
