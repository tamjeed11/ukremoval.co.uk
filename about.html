Our Story

A Removal Company Built on One Idea: Moving Shouldn't Be Stressful

UK Removal & Logistics was started in London in 2024 by a team of people who were unhappy with removal companies that did not show up on time, damaged belongings, or charged unexpected fees.

We got tired of seeing people have bad experiences. So we built something different. We tell you the price, and that is the price you pay. Our team arrives on time, and we handle your belongings like they are our own.

In a little over a year, we have completed over 500 moves across the UK. We are still growing, because happy customers tell their friends about us. That is the best way for us to get more customers.
