FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about moving with UK Removal & Logistics.

How quickly can you arrange a move?

In many cases we can arrange same-day or next-day moves. For larger moves we typically need 2–3 days' notice. Call or WhatsApp us and we'll do our best to accommodate your timeline.

Are you fully insured?

Yes. We carry full goods-in-transit insurance and public liability insurance on every single move, giving you complete peace of mind from start to finish.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes boxes, bubble wrap, furniture blankets, and specialist wrapping for delicate items. These are all included as part of our packing service, or available to purchase separately.

What areas do you cover?

We cover the entire UK London, South East, Midlands, North, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, just give us a call and we'll confirm straight away.

Will there be any hidden charges?

Never. The price we quote is the price you pay. Full stop. We believe in complete transparency no surprise charges, no last-minute additions.

Can you move items into storage?

Yes we offer flexible storage with secure, climate-controlled units. We handle collection, storage, and redelivery all in one seamless service.

Do you offer same-day and weekend moves?

Yes! We're available 7 days a week, including weekends and bank holidays. Same-day moves are available subject to availability contact us as early as possible and we'll do our best to help.

How do I get a quote?

Simply fill in our quick online quote form, give us a call on 020 3084 3562, or send us a WhatsApp message at +44 7754 844481. We'll come back to you within minutes during opening hours with a fixed, no-obligation price.

Can you disassemble and reassemble furniture?

Yes. Our team can disassemble items like beds, wardrobes, and flat-pack furniture before the move, and reassemble them at your new property. Just let us know when you book.

What should I do to prepare for moving day?

If you're not using our full packing service, we recommend boxing up small items in advance, labelling boxes by room, and making sure the team has clear access to both properties. We'll handle all the heavy lifting and large furniture.

Do you handle fragile or valuable items?

Absolutely. We take extra care with antiques, artwork, electronics, mirrors, and other fragile items. We use specialist wrapping materials and ensure everything is secured properly in the vehicle.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer, card payment, and cash. Payment terms are confirmed at the time of booking. Contact us if you have any specific requirements.

Still have questions?

Our team is available 7 days a week. Get in touch and we'll answer any questions you have.
