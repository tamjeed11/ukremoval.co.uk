2. Definitions

  • "Customer" means the individual or organisation booking our services.
  • "Services" means all removal, packing, storage, and related services we provide.
  • "Quote" means the written or digital estimate provided to the Customer before booking.
  • "Booking" means the confirmed arrangement of our Services for a specific date.

3. Quotations & Pricing

All quotes provided are fixed prices based on the information you supply. If the actual move differs significantly from what was described (e.g. additional items, restricted access not disclosed, or significantly greater distance), we reserve the right to adjust the price before commencing work.

Prices include VAT where applicable. Payment is due upon completion of the move unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4. Bookings & Confirmation

A booking is confirmed once you accept our quote in writing (including email, WhatsApp, or via our online form) and we acknowledge acceptance. We will send you a confirmation with all relevant details including date, time, and price.

5. Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the scheduled move date incur no charge.
  • Cancellations made within 48 hours of the scheduled move may incur a cancellation fee of up to 50% of the quoted price.
  • Cancellations on the day of the move may be charged at the full quoted price.
  • Rescheduling is free of charge subject to availability and provided at least 48 hours' notice is given.

6. Customer Responsibilities

The Customer must:

  • Provide accurate information about the volume and nature of items to be moved.
  • Disclose any access restrictions, parking limitations, or building regulations at either location.
  • Ensure clear and safe access for our team and vehicles.
  • Declare any high-value items (over £1,000 individual value) before the move.
  • Ensure all items are legal to transport.

7. Insurance & Liability

We carry comprehensive goods-in-transit insurance and public liability insurance on every move. Our liability is subject to the following:

  • We will take all reasonable care in handling your belongings.
  • Claims for damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery in writing.
  • We are not liable for damage to items that were inadequately packed by the Customer (unless we provided the packing service).
  • We are not liable for damage caused by inherent defects, natural deterioration, or items not disclosed to us.
  • Our maximum liability for any single item is limited to £500 unless a higher value is declared and agreed in writing before the move.
  • We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses.

8. Items We Cannot Move

We cannot transport hazardous materials, illegal substances, perishable goods (unless agreed in writing), live animals, or items of extraordinary value without prior arrangement and additional insurance.

9. Access & Delays

If our team is unable to access the property or complete the move due to circumstances beyond our control (e.g. road closures, parking restrictions, lift breakdowns, or keys not available), we may need to reschedule. Additional charges may apply if significant waiting time or return visits are required.
