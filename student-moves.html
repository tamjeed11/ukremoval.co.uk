About This Service

Student-Friendly Moving

Moving between university accommodation, shared houses, or back home for the holidays can be stressful. This is especially true when you're managing exams, deadlines, and a tight budget. Our student removal service is created just for students. We offer affordable prices and flexible options to meet your needs.

We know that student moves are usually smaller than full house removals. That's why we provide tailored pricing for single rooms and shared accommodation. Whether you're moving from halls to a shared house, between student properties, or back home at the end of term, we offer a fast, reliable, and budget-friendly service.

We also have short-term storage solutions for students who need to keep their belongings over the summer or between tenancies. Our secure, climate-controlled units are available on flexible monthly contracts, and we can collect and deliver your items whenever you need them.
