FAQ

Home Removals FAQ

Common questions about our house removal service.

How long does a home removal take?

A typical 1–2 bedroom home removal takes 3–6 hours. Larger 3–4 bedroom homes can take a full day. Factors like distance, number of floors, and whether packing is included all affect the time. We'll give you a realistic estimate when you book so you can plan your day accordingly.

Do you disassemble and reassemble furniture?

Yes. Our team can disassemble beds, wardrobes, dining tables, and flat-pack furniture before the move and reassemble everything at your new home. Just let us know when booking so we can allocate the right time and tools. There's no extra charge for standard disassembly and reassembly.

What if my move date changes?

We understand that moving dates can shift especially when chains are involved. We'll do our best to accommodate date changes with as much notice as possible. Contact us as soon as you know and we'll rearrange your booking. We aim to be as flexible as possible during what can be a stressful time.

Do you move to and from flats?

Absolutely. We regularly move to and from flats, including high-rise buildings and properties with no lift access. We handle stair carries and liaise with building management for lift access where needed. Just let us know about any access restrictions when you book so we can plan accordingly.

Are my belongings insured during the move?

Yes. We carry full goods-in-transit insurance on every move. Your belongings are covered from the moment we load them onto the van to the moment they're placed in your new home. We also carry public liability insurance. If you have particularly high-value items, let us know and we can discuss additional cover options.
