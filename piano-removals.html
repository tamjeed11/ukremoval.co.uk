About This Service

Expert Piano Moving

Pianos are really heavy and delicate, and they are also very valuable. So when you need to move a piano, you need to know what you are doing. You need the right equipment and a lot of experience. Our piano removal service is built for moving pianos like upright pianos and grand pianos. We can move them across the street or all the way across the country.

We have the tools to move pianos safely, including dollies and blankets made just for pianos. Our team knows how to lift pianos properly and how to get them around stairs and tight spots without damaging the piano or your property.

We have insurance that covers every piano move so you do not have to worry. We take care of your piano like it is our own. We have moved a lot of pianos, from smaller uprights to really big concert grands. Pianos are important instruments, so we make sure to protect both the outside and the inside throughout the move.
