About This Service

Your Trusted Office Relocation Partner

Every hour that your office is closed costs you money. That is why our office removal service is designed to minimize disruption and get your team back to work as quickly as possible. Whether you are moving a startup or a large corporate office with many floors, we plan and execute every detail carefully.

We know that moving an office is more complicated than moving a home. You have to protect your computers and other equipment, handle documents securely, and meet tight deadlines. Our project managers work with you beforehand to create a plan for the move so nothing goes wrong on the day.

Most of the time we move offices at night or on weekends so we do not disrupt your work. Our team arrives after your office closes and has everything set up and ready at your new location before your staff arrive the next morning. We label every item, including cables and furniture, so it is easy to put everything back together quickly and correctly.

We operate across the United Kingdom, from London and the South East to Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, and beyond. Our removal vehicles are specially designed for this purpose and carry the right materials to pack computers and other sensitive equipment safely. We also have secure boxes for documents. Every office move is fully insured so you are protected against accidents and losses.

FAQ

Office Removals FAQ

Do you do out-of-hours office moves?

Yes. We regularly carry out office relocations overnight and at weekends to minimise disruption to your business. We work around your schedule, not the other way around.

Can you handle IT equipment safely?

Absolutely. Our team is trained to safely disconnect, pack, transport, and reconnect IT equipment including servers, monitors, workstations, and networking hardware. We use specialist anti-static packing materials throughout.

How much does an office removal cost?

Office removals start from £250. The final price depends on the size of your office, the distance of the move, and whether out-of-hours service is required. We provide a fixed, all-inclusive quote after an initial consultation.

Do you provide a dedicated project manager?

Yes. Every office relocation is assigned a dedicated project manager who coordinates the entire move from the initial site visit and planning through to post-move follow-up. You'll have one point of contact throughout.
