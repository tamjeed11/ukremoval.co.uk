Google Ads Marketing Proposal
This proposal outlines a strategic Google Ads marketing plan designed to increase qualified leads, phone calls,
and bookings for a removals and relocation business operating within the UK market.
Project Objectives
• Generate high-quality removal service leads
• Increase inbound calls and quote requests
• Improve local search visibility in targeted UK cities
• Reduce wasted ad spend through optimized targeting
• Build long-term scalable lead generation
Recommended Advertising Strategy
1. Google Search Campaigns
Target users actively searching for moving and relocation services.
2. Call-Only Campaigns
Designed to increase direct phone inquiries from mobile users.
3. Remarketing Campaigns
Re-engage website visitors who did not initially convert.
4. Location-Based Campaigns
Separate campaigns for high-value areas including London, Birmingham, Manchester, and surrounding regions.
Target Keywords Examples
• House removals near me
• Office relocation services
• Moving company UK
• Furniture movers London
• Same day removals
• Affordable removals service
Recommended Monthly Ad Budget
Starter Campaign: £800 – £1,500/month
Suitable for local testing and small-scale lead generation.
Growth Campaign: £2,000 – £5,000/month
Recommended for consistent lead flow and multi-city targeting.
Scale Campaign: £7,000+/month
Recommended for aggressive expansion and high-volume lead acquisition.
Campaign Timeline
Week 1–2:
• Google Ads account setup
• Keyword research
• Competitor analysis
• Conversion tracking setup
• Audience targeting setup
Month 1:
• Campaign testing and optimization
• Search term analysis
• Ad performance monitoring
Month 2–3:
• Scaling profitable campaigns
• Reducing cost per lead
• Improving conversion quality
Landing Page Recommendations
To maximize conversion rates from paid advertising traffic, the following improvements are strongly
recommended:
• Dedicated landing pages for each service
• Dedicated pages for each city/location
• Fast mobile performance optimization
• Strong call-to-action sections
• Integrated customer reviews and testimonials
• Sticky call button for mobile users
• Proper analytics and conversion tracking

Monthly Management Fee:

Final Recommendation
A minimum testing period of 90 days is recommended to gather accurate campaign data, optimize targeting, and
establish stable lead generation performance. For competitive UK markets, a realistic starting budget should be
between £1,500 – £2,500 per month to achieve meaningful and scalable results.
