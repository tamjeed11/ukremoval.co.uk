About This Service

Expert Packing for Your Move

Packing can be a real headache when you're moving. Our professional packers take care of everything for you. Whether we're packing your whole home or just the fragile items you're worried about, our team handles everything with care.

We supply all the packing materials you need, including boxes in different sizes, bubble wrap, packing paper, tape, and special wrapping for fragile items. Our team knows how to pack efficiently, protecting your belongings while making the most of space. We label every box clearly with what's inside and where it goes in your new home, so unpacking is as simple as possible.

We use specialist packing techniques and materials for fragile items like glassware, artwork, mirrors, and electronics. We've packed thousands of homes and know how to keep even the most delicate items safe during the move. Everything we pack is covered by our insurance.
